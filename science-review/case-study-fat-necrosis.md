# Science Review: Fat Necrosis

**Review of "Fat Necrosis" Article**

1. **Location:** Intro section
   - **Issue:** Conflation of enzymatic and traumatic fat necrosis, implying both involve enzymes when traumatic does not.
   - **Correction:** Clarify that traumatic fat necrosis results from direct adipocyte rupture, not enzyme activity.

2. **Location:** Process > Why Lipase...
   - **Issue:** Claims hypocalcemia is due to saponification; more likely caused by ATP depletion and bone mobilization issues.
   - **Correction:** Note that hypocalcemia in pancreatitis is primarily from ATP depletion and bone resistance, not saponification alone.

3. **Location:** Differential of White Lesions
   - **Issue:** Omissions of pseudomyxoma peritonei and calcified lymph nodes as differentials.
   - **Correction:** Add these conditions to the differential diagnosis list for completeness.

4. **Location:** Traumatic Fat Necrosis section
   - **Issue:** Incorrectly attributes traumatic necrosis to tissue lipases instead of pancreatic enzymes.
   - **Correction:** Emphasize that traumatic cases lack enzyme involvement, distinguishing them from enzymatic causes.

5. **Location:** Outcome section
   - **Issue:** States calcium soaps are inert and resorbed; underestimates potential for complications like abscesses.
   - **Correction:** Acknowledge possible complications beyond simple resolution.

**Conclusion:** The article has several factual inaccuracies and omissions that could lead to misdiagnosis or misunderstanding. Corrections are needed to accurately distinguish between types of fat necrosis, provide accurate causes of hypocalcemia, and include all relevant differential diagnoses.
