# Science Review: Action Potential

- **Location:** Outcome section
- **Issue:** The claim that flecainide slows recovery from inactivation might be oversimplified or incorrect. Flecainide primarily acts by blocking voltage-gated sodium channels rather than affecting the recovery process.
- **Correction:** Flecainide works by selectively binding to the activated state of sodium channels, thereby reducing the duration of the action potential and refractory period, rather than slowing recovery from inactivation.

No other accuracy issues found.
