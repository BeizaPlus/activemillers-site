# Science Review: Actinic Keratosis

- **Location:** Intro paragraph
- **Issue:** The article inaccurately states that actinic keratosis begins in the stratum corneum when the primary damage occurs in the basal layer due to UV-induced DNA mutations.
- **Correction:** Actinic keratosis originates from UV-induced damage in the basal layer, where TP53 mutations occur, leading to epidermal changes visible in higher layers.
