AL Amyloidosis

Immunoglobulin light chain amyloid. An abnormal clone of plasma cells produces excess monoclonal light chains, which circulate and physically occlude the glomerular capillary lumen — blood backs up at the blockage, the glomerulus loses its filtering capacity, and the kidney itself shrinks into a small, diffusely scarred organ: end-stage renal failure. Traditionally called "primary" amyloidosis, though a proportion of patients have overt multiple myeloma.
