Complications of Atheroma

A critically narrowed coronary artery's fibrous cap splits under pulsatile trauma — plaque fissuring — letting blood track into the plaque and narrow the lumen even further. The exposed, thrombogenic plaque surface then builds a thrombus of fibrin and platelets, largely obliterating the vessel. This exact sequence, in the left anterior descending coronary artery, is what the book's own case ends in fatal myocardial infarction.
