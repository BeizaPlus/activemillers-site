Cryptococcus

One organism, two very different outcomes. In an immunosuppressed host, Cryptococcus forms "soap bubble" cysts in the brain with almost no inflammatory response — its thick polysaccharide capsule inhibits phagocytosis. In an immunocompetent host, the same organism provokes a real granulomatous fight.
