Fibroadenoma: Compressive Physics

A fibroadenoma is a biphasic fibroepithelial lesion. Its ducts sit within a collagen-rich stroma that can expand and physically reshape the more compliant epithelial spaces.

This short visual case connects the real histology to a three-dimensional interpretation of that compression. The complete interactive case will continue to grow here.
